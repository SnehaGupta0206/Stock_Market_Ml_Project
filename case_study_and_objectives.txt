Case Study

Project: Forecasting Indian Stock Market Indices using Machine Learning
The Story

For eleven years the firm's flagship product has been the "Daily Market Outlook" —
a morning note on India's three key NSE indices (NIFTY 50, NIFTY BANK, NIFTY MIDCAP),
written entirely from the experience and intuition of one veteran analyst.

This Monday, an email from the firm's oldest client lands on the desk of Priya Menon,
Head of Research:

> "Your note said NIFTY BANK would 'remain range-bound with a positive bias.' It fell
> nearly 4% in two sessions. On what evidence was that view based? We are
> reconsidering our subscription."

Priya's decision is instant: no view leaves the office without data behind it — starting today.
She hands the assignment to her newest data-science intern (me), along with a licensed
research archive of twelve years of NSE index data —
[Forecasting of Indian Stock Market Indices, Mendeley Data, doi:10.17632/swrw7d78ss.1,
CC BY 4.0](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/swrw7d78ss/1):
~8,200 trading days (2013 → 2024), 20 columns per day — previous day's Open/High/Low/
Close/Volume and 14 technical indicators — with one target: today's closing price
Close_(t).

Her brief is one sentence: "Explain how these indices actually behave, prove which
signals genuinely work, and tell me where tomorrow's close is heading."


Project Objectives (the insights we set out to extract)

Objective 1 — Wealth creation over the long run
Identify (which index rewarded investors the most between 2013–2024), and how each of
the three behaved through rallies, corrections, and the 2020 crash-and-recovery —
revealing whether large-cap stability, banking intensity, or mid-cap growth built the
most wealth.

Objective 2 — The true risk personality of each index
Quantify (which index is genuinely the riskiest and exactly when market risk spikes),
by exposing the volatility regimes hidden inside daily returns — turning the veterans'
"feelings" about risk into measurable fact.

Objective 3 — The predictive power of trading volume
Determine (whether heavy trading volume contains an early signal for the size and
direction of the next price move) — confirming or retiring the classic trader's rule
"volume confirms the trend" with evidence from twelve years of data.

Objective 4 — Which technical indicators genuinely earn their place
Separate the (few indicators that truly carry signal about the closing price) from
the ones that are noise or merely repeat each other — discovering which 3–5 signals an
analyst should actually watch every morning.

Objective 5 — How far can machines see into tomorrow?
Establish (how accurately the next day's closing price and direction can be predicted),
and identify the single most reliable machine-learning model for the job —
the forecast that will anchor every future Daily Market Outlook.
