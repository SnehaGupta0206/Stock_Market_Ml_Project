Only Logistic Regression beat the naive baseline. Decision Tree (53.9% test vs 100% train, depth 40) and Random Forest (100% train) memorised market noise — overfitting, proven.

Key findings

  • Wealth: MIDCAP grew 6.9× vs NIFTY50's 3.9× (2013–2024).
  • Risk: MIDCAP ~32% more volatile daily - reward and risk arrive together.
  • Volume myth: heavy volume alone does not predict the next move (corr ≈ 0).
  • Indicators: SMA/EMA/SAR track price levels (corr ~1.0); oscillators (RSI, Stochastic) carry the direction signal - feature selection on them improved every model.
  • Best-fit: Logistic Regression (61.3%) - simple beat complex on noisy market data.
  • Honesty check: daily direction is near a coin toss; even a small edge over the baseline is a professional-grade result.
  • Data governance: the BANKNIFTY vendor file failed High≥Low in 97% of rows and was excluded before any modelling.

Recommendations

  • Desk use: treat the LR compass as a supporting signal, always paired with risk limits in high-volatility regimes.
  • Client advice: MIDCAP for long-horizon growth mandates; NIFTY50 for risk-sensitive clients.
  • Process: keep the integrity checks as a permanent gate for every new data vendor file.
  • Next versions: walk-forward monthly validation; add news-sentiment & macro features; test weekly horizons where signal is stronger.
